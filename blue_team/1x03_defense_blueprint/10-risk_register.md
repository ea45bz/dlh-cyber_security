#### Risk Register Governance Note

The MedDefense risk register is **maintained by the Deputy CISO (James Chen) in collaboration with the IT Director (Sarah Park)**, who act as primary custodians. The register is reviewed **monthly** during the formal security review meeting; each risk owner must update ALE figures and KRI metrics before that meeting. Out‑of‑cycle reviews are triggered by: new threat intelligence (e.g., a CISA advisory), significant changes to the asset inventory, regulatory audit findings, or any KRI exceeding its defined threshold. When a KRI breach occurs, the associated risk owner must convene an urgent incident review with the security team and the CISO, reassess ALE, accelerate the planned controls, and notify senior leadership if the residual risk score rises above a critical level.
