## Explain how CWE taxonomy helps in vulnerability assessment and risk management.

The CWE taxonomy turns abstract vulnerability concepts into concrete, trackable categories.

## What are the benefits of using a standardized classification system like CWE

It makes assessment systematic, risk prioritization data‑driven, and remediation consistent across teams and projects.
It’s the “glue” that ties code reviews, scanning tools, compliance audits, and business risk discussions together under a single, universally understood framework.
